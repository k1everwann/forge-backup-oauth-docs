Forge Backup Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026.

Forge Backup is a personal utility used only by its owner. Google Drive authorization is used to locate, read, write, and manage encrypted backup archives and their metadata, including retention of older backups.

Backup contents are encrypted before upload. OAuth credentials are stored in restricted configuration files on the owner's devices. Google stores and processes Drive data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Forge Backup does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or disclose it to other users. Data is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The owner controls retention and may revoke authorization through Google Account permissions. Revoking access does not automatically delete previously uploaded backups. This documentation site does not operate a backup service for visitors.

For support, use the developer support contact shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
